Question 1: Mathematics

If a train travels at 80 km/h for 2.5 hours, how far does it travel?

Options:

A) 180 km

B) 200 km

C) 220 km

D) 240 km

Answer: B) 200 km
Explanation: Distance = Speed × Time = 80 km/h × 2.5 h = 200 km

Question 2: Chemistry

What is the chemical symbol for gold?

Options:

A) Ag

B) Au

C) Fe

D) Pb

Answer: B) Au
Explanation: Au is the chemical symbol for gold from Latin "aurum"
