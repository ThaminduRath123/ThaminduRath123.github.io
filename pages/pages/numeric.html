Question 1

Solve for x: 3x + 7 = 22

Answer: 5
Explanation: 3x = 22 - 7 = 15, therefore x = 15 ÷ 3 = 5

Question 2

What is the area of a circle with radius 7 cm? (Use π = 3.14)

Answer: 153.86 cm²
Explanation: Area = πr² = 3.14 × 7² = 3.14 × 49 = 153.86
